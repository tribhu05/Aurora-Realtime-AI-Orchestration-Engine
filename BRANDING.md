# Aurora — Branding & Trademark Guidelines

**Original Creator**: Tanuj  
**Project**: Aurora — Interruptible Voice AI Assistant  
**Year**: 2026

---

## 1. Overview & Purpose

The **Aurora** project was conceived, designed, and engineered by **Tanuj** to advance responsive, interruptible voice-first artificial intelligence.

While the source code is made available for study, personal learning, and non-commercial development under the custom [Aurora Source-Available License](LICENSE), the **name, visual identity, logos, and branding** represent the reputation and original craftsmanship of the creator.

These guidelines define how the Aurora name, artwork, and identity may—and may not—be used by third parties, contributors, forks, and derivative projects.

---

## 2. Brand Identity vs. Source Code License

1. **No Trademark License**: The [LICENSE](LICENSE) governing the source code grants copyright permissions only. It **does not** grant any license, title, or right to use the project's name, brand marks, visual design, or authorial identity for commercial or promotional purposes.
2. **Legal Status of Brand**: This policy protects common-law trade identity, project reputation, and consumer clarity against deceptive practices or confusion. This document makes no claim of federally or internationally registered trademark status unless and until such registration is formally finalized.
3. **Prevention of Confusion**: The primary objective of these guidelines is to ensure that users, developers, and consumers can immediately distinguish between:
   - The **official Aurora project** authored and maintained by Tanuj; and
   - Third-party forks, independent adaptations, or community experiments.

---

## 3. Project Naming Guidelines for Forks & Derivatives

If you fork, adapt, or build upon the Aurora codebase, you **must select a distinct name** for your distribution or deployment.

### ✅ Permitted Naming Conventions

- `[YourProjectName]` (an entirely unique name)
- `[YourProjectName] (built with the Aurora voice core)`
- `[YourProjectName] — based on Aurora by Tanuj`
- `[YourProjectName] (an experimental fork of Aurora)`

### ❌ Prohibited Naming Conventions

- `Aurora` or `Aurora AI` (cannot be used as the primary title of a fork)
- `Aurora Pro`, `Aurora Plus`, `Aurora Enterprise`, `Aurora Ultra`
- `Aurora Official`, `Aurora Community Edition`
- `Aurora 2.0` or `Aurora Next`
- Any name that incorporates "Aurora" in a manner that creates consumer confusion or implies official authorization, partnership, or succession.

---

## 4. Visual Assets & Logo Usage

1. **Logos and Icons**: The Aurora 3D reactive orb styling, custom SVGs, favicon, and graphical badges may not be used as the primary brand logo for any third-party product, service, or commercial offering.
2. **Screenshots & Demonstrations**: You may include unmodified screenshots or screen recordings of the official Aurora UI in educational articles, conference presentations, academic research papers, or non-commercial portfolio reviews, provided you conspicuously attribute the project to **Tanuj**.
3. **UI Reskinning Recommended**: For independent public services or apps based on Aurora, we encourage designing a distinct visual theme and user interface to highlight your own creative contributions.

---

## 5. Endorsement & Affiliation

1. **No Implied Affiliation**: You must not state or imply that your fork, product, company, or tutorial is sponsored, endorsed, maintained, or approved by **Tanuj** or the Aurora project.
2. **Factual Statements Allowed**: You are welcome to make accurate, factual statements regarding your technical foundation, such as:
   > _"This voice assistant utilizes orchestration concepts and architectural code derived from Aurora, created by Tanuj."_
3. **Author Credit for Your Work**: You are fully entitled and encouraged to take clear, unambiguous credit for your own modifications, architectural extensions, and original code. Do not hide your authorship—simply distinguish it from the original Aurora foundation.
