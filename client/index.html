Microphone Setup for Android

Chrome on Android routes voice through Android's OS Speech Engine. If Chrome has permission but says blocked:

1
Google Speech Services Permission

In phone Settings > Apps > Google (or Speech Services by Google) > Permissions > Microphone → Set to Allow only while using the app.

2
Global Android Mic Access Toggle

Swipe down twice from top of phone screen. Ensure the green "Mic access" privacy tile is turned ON.

3
Chrome Site Setting & HTTPS

Tap the 🔒 lock icon in Chrome's URL bar > Permissions > Microphone → Allowed. Ensure you are on the secure HTTPS live link.
